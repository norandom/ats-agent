# Worked embedded review

This case uses wholly fictional facts. The host authored the prose and reviewed its meaning; deterministic tests do not establish editorial quality. Optional COBESY and Humanizer are assumed unavailable for this exercise. Their execution records must stay false. The host applied the embedded criteria below, without installing or invoking either optional skill.

The target is a dispatch role that requires manifest reconciliation and reporting routing exceptions. The source supports two historical actions: reconciling parcel manifests and reporting routing exceptions. It contains no outcome metric, approval authority, employer culture evidence or proof of current tool proficiency.

## Draft and factual audit

Weak draft: "A passionate professional with experience in manifests, routing exceptions and operational excellence."

The useful contribution is manifest reconciliation. The inventory hides both supported actions behind topic nouns. "Passionate" and "operational excellence" add unsupported emphasis; the source does not establish either. The employer's decision is whether to discuss relevant dispatch experience. Keep the two concrete actions and avoid implying that reporting an exception resolved it.

Proposed final English wording: "Reconciled parcel manifests and reported routing exceptions."

Proposed final German wording: "Glich Paketlisten ab und meldete Abweichungen von den vorgesehenen Routen."

The host reviewed both against the same fictional source. "Glich Paketlisten ab" preserves manifest reconciliation. "Meldete Abweichungen von den vorgesehenen Routen" describes reporting routing exceptions without assigning them to a planning stage. The source does not establish when those exceptions arose or who planned the routes. Neither version adds resolution authority, impact or recency. Each has one short sentence with ordinary punctuation and no em or en dashes. In a full draft, the two propositions need separate claim IDs and exact occurrence spans under the existing composition contract. The final wording groups the evidence around relevant work rather than listing tools. A private contribution thesis can explain its relevance to dispatch accuracy, but cannot claim measured improvement.
